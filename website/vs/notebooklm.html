AnchorCore vs NotebookLM: a local, private alternative

People search for a "NotebookLM alternative" for two different reasons: they want something that runs on their own machine, or they want it to read live sources such as a folder, Jira or Linear instead of uploaded files. AnchorCore is built for those two cases. NotebookLM is better at others, and this page says which.

A naming note. Google announced on July 16, 2026 that it is renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook, calling it "the same standalone product" (Google's announcement). We say NotebookLM here because that is what people still search for.

When AnchorCore is the better fit

  • The documents must not go to a hosted service. With a local Ollama model, indexing, search and answers run on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, and with Wi-Fi off folder sources still work. With a cloud model you choose, your question and the retrieved passages go to that provider; the labels let you keep chosen sources out of that path.
  • Your knowledge lives in a folder or a tracker, and changes. A folder source re-syncs when files change, and Jira, Linear and Drive are connectors rather than copies you re-upload.
  • You want your own model. Run a small local model, or point it at the OpenAI-compatible endpoint your company already approves.
  • You use coding agents. The same cited search is available to them over MCP.
  • You want to check the claims. The code is public and source-available; personal use is free and team or commercial use needs a license. See what it sends and where.

When NotebookLM is the better fit

  • You want zero setup. It is a hosted service: no download, no local model.
  • You want audio overviews. AnchorCore does not generate audio.
  • Your sources are web pages, YouTube videos, audio or images. AnchorCore has no web-page, video or audio source type, and it does not read what is inside an image: pictures are kept and shown next to the text that refers to them, but answers come from text. It reads files in a folder and the connectors listed above.
  • Your material already lives in Google Docs and Slides and the content is fine to keep in Google's cloud.
  • You need heavy collaboration or SSO today. AnchorCore Team has optional password sign-in only, with no roles and no SSO.
