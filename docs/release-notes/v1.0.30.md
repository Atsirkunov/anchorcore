# AnchorCore 1.0.30

A correctness release for search, driven by a field report: paging,
score scales, and contents-page handling all misbehaved in large
collections. No new features, no migration.

## Fixed

- **Search `limit` was silently capped at 8.** The page size paginates
  over the retrieved candidate list, but retrieval depth defaulted to 8
  no matter what `limit` you asked for — `limit: 50` still returned 7
  hits. Retrieval now widens to match the requested page.
- **Scores mixed two incompatible scales.** Results pulled in through
  keyword slots carried raw keyword scores (~14) next to fused scores
  (~0.01), making the `score` field meaningless to compare — including
  for agents reading the API. Slot hits now score on the same scale as
  everything else.
- **Contents pages still produced ghost sections.** The 1.0.29 fix only
  caught dense, short table-of-contents runs. Sparse layouts (entries
  separated by blank lines, the common shape in extracted PDFs) and
  entries padded with dot leaders past 80 characters slipped through.
  Both are now caught.

## Upgrade notes

- No database migration and no re-index required for the search fixes —
  they apply immediately.
- **Ghost-section cleanup needs a force re-sync.** Contents-page
  suppression runs when documents are chunked, and routine syncs skip
  unchanged files — already-indexed documents keep their ghost sections
  until you Reclassify the source.
- Larger pages retrieve more before ranking: `limit: 50` now genuinely
  scores 50 candidates. Expect slightly slower responses on very large
  pages; the cap remains 50.
