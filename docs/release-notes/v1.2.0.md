# AnchorCore 1.2.0

A new look for the app and the website, a clearer Ask screen with
citations you can follow, a light and dark theme switch, and cleaner
answers from small local models.

## Highlights

- **A new look, shared with anchorcore.dev.** The app now matches the
  website: ink on paper, IBM Plex type, and one accent colour reserved
  for citations and links. The fonts ship inside the app, so it looks
  the same offline.
- **Light, dark, or follow your system.** A new Auto / Light / Dark
  switch sits in the app header (and on the website). Auto follows
  your operating system; your choice is remembered.
- **An Ask screen built around citations.** Each question now reads
  above its answer. Answers render paragraphs, lists and bold text,
  and every `[S1]` marker points at a numbered source card — hover a
  marker to highlight its source. Source cards show the section, the
  file path relative to your folder, the line range and one clean
  excerpt, instead of raw text, absolute paths and scores.
- **Agent mode shows its evidence the same way.** When your AI agent
  writes the answers, the Ask screen shows the same source cards as
  "Evidence for your agent" — exactly what the agent receives.

## Fixed

- **No more `[match 7]` in answers.** Small local models (such as
  llama3.2:3b) copied internal match markers into their answers,
  sometimes without the brackets. The answer model no longer sees
  them; agents still receive them over MCP and REST, as before.
- **Cleaner screens.** Emoji icons and coloured stripe borders are
  gone; statuses are written out in words.

## Upgrade notes

- No database migrations and no setting changes. Your theme starts on
  Auto (follows your system).
- The app embeds its interface at build time — use the 1.2.0 download,
  not an older build, to see the new screens.
