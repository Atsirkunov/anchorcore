# AnchorCore 1.6.1

1.6.0 could not start on a database created by an earlier version: the
upgrade stopped with an error and the app quit at launch. 1.6.1 fixes the
upgrade. New installs were not affected.

## Fixed

- **Upgrading from 1.5.x or older failed at launch.** The 1.6.0 upgrade step
  that turns the old per-document PII mark into the new Sensitive override
  hit a database constraint (`NOT NULL constraint failed:
  ingested_items.sensitive_override`) and the app exited. The failed upgrade
  rolled back completely, so your data is untouched — install 1.6.1 and
  launch again. Documents you had explicitly marked keep their mark as
  Sensitive; everything else inherits from its source, as 1.6.0 intended.

## Upgrade notes

- **The first launch on a large database takes a while.** The upgrade
  rewrites the chunk table once (and, coming from 1.4.x or older, also makes
  a safety backup and cleans up legacy entities). On a multi-gigabyte
  database expect several minutes before the window opens; the app is
  working, not hung. Later launches are fast.
- **Coming from 1.4.x or older and you tried 1.6.0?** Each failed launch
  left a full copy of your database in `exports/` inside the data directory
  (`safety-backup-pre-r45.10-*.db`). They are never removed automatically —
  keep the newest one until 1.6.1 has started successfully, then delete the
  extras to reclaim the space.
